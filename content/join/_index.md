## Current Postdoctoral Openings

### Postdoctoral Scholar in Single-Cell Genomics

The Wang group is recruiting a postdoctoral scholar to develop statistical and machine learning methods for single-cell perturbation data. Human genetic studies can implicate a gene in disease while leaving open which cell types it affects and which molecular pathways are involved. We use perturbation experiments to investigate these questions.

One direction is to combine Perturb-seq measurements with single-cell atlases and gene representations learned by pretrained AI models. The postdoc could use these data to predict how different cell types respond to untested perturbations, evaluate the predictions against independent experiments, and quantify their uncertainty.

This work is part of our [NIH R35 research program]({{< relref "/post/R35_2026.md" >}}). To inquire about the position, email [Jingshu Wang](mailto:jingshuw@uchicago.edu).

### Postdoctoral Scholar in Computational Neurogenomics  
*(Joint position with Xiaochang Zhang, University of Chicago)*

This joint position focuses on the computational and quantitative analysis of single-cell and long-read transcriptomic data to investigate gene regulation and RNA processing in neural development. The project combines neurogenomics with principled statistical modeling and functional genomics, with opportunities to contribute to both methodological innovation and biological discovery.
