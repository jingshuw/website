María Valdez Cabrera joined the University of Chicago on November 1 as a postdoctoral scholar working jointly with Jingshu Wang and Matthew Stephens. María comes to Chicago from the University of Washington, where she developed statistical methods for comparing phylogenetic trees. Welcome, María!
