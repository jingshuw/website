Our team, Team Outlier, placed **third** in the 2025 Virtual Cell Challenge. In its [2025 wrap-up](https://arcinstitute.org/news/virtual-cell-challenge-2025-wrap-up), Arc Institute cited our [paper on the Perturbation Discrimination Score (PDS)](/publication/liu-2025-aa/).

Congratulations to **Qiyuan** for her major contributions to this effort!

**November 19, 2026 update.** We showed that simply rescaling a model’s predictions could increase its L1-based PDS. Arc cited this finding as the reason it switched to a cosine-based PDS for the 2026 challenge. [Read Arc’s 2026 wrap-up](https://arcinstitute.org/news/virtual-cell-challenge-2026-wrap-up).
