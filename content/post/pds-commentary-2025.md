We posted a short commentary on the **Perturbation Discrimination Score (PDS)** used in the Virtual Cell Challenge. [Read the commentary on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16954).

The note discusses why early improvements in PDS can be unexpectedly difficult to achieve, and how scaling can lead to significant gains by making the usual ℓ₁/ℓ₂-based PDS behave more like a cosine-similarity–based score, which is often more discriminative.

**November 19, 2026 update.** Arc Institute cited our commentary in its 2025 challenge wrap-up. In 2026, the organizers credited our finding that rescaling predictions can increase the L1-based PDS as the reason they switched to a cosine-based score.
