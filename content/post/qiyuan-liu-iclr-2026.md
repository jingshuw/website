Qiyuan Liu presented “Effects of Distance Metrics and Scaling on the Perturbation Discrimination Score” at the ICLR 2026 Workshop on Generative AI in Genomics. The organizers selected the paper for one of three oral spotlights.

Qiyuan showed how changing the scale of a model’s predictions or the distance used to compare them can change its score when evaluating predicted gene-expression responses. Congratulations, Qiyuan!
